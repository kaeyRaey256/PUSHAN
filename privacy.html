Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026

Pushan Enterprise (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

We operate in accordance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Pushan Enterprise is a strategic market linkage and supply chain advisory firm registered and operating in Uganda.

  • Trading name: Pushan Enterprise
  • Address: Plot 104–114 Butabika Road, Kampala, Uganda
  • Contact: office@pushanenterprise.com

2. What data we collect

We collect personal data only when you choose to provide it. This occurs primarily when you complete the enquiry form on our website. Data collected may include your name, email address, country of operation, the nature of your enquiry, and any information you include in your message. We do not collect sensitive personal data, payment information, or data from children under 18.

3. How we use your data

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, assess whether and how Pushan Enterprise can assist you, and communicate with you about our services where requested. We do not use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your enquiry.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of legitimate interests — specifically, to respond to enquiries submitted by individuals who have chosen to contact us. Where required by law, we rely on consent.

5. Retention

We retain enquiry data for a maximum of 24 months from the date of receipt, unless an ongoing business relationship requires longer retention. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information with email service providers used to manage enquiries, bound by appropriate data processing agreements, and with legal or regulatory authorities where required by law.

7. Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts. When the site loads, your browser may make a request to Google’s servers to retrieve font files, which may include your IP address and browser information. Google’s privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not use analytics cookies or tracking technologies on this website.

8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at office@pushanenterprise.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but take our obligations seriously.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
