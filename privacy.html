Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026

Pushan Enterprise (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to the protection of personal information in accordance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Pushan Enterprise is a Strategic Market Linkage and Supply Chain Advisory firm registered and operating in Uganda.

  • Address: Plot 104–114 Butabika Road, Kampala, Uganda
  • Contact: office@pushanenterprise.com

2. Data collected

We collect personal data only when you choose to provide it via the enquiry form on our website. This includes your name, email address, country of operation, commodity or sector, and the content of your message. We collect no sensitive personal data, payment information, or data from persons under 18.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is used solely to respond to enquiries and assess whether Pushan Enterprise can assist. Our legal basis for processing is legitimate interests — responding to enquiries you have initiated. Where required by applicable law, we rely on consent.

4. Retention

Enquiry data is retained for a maximum of 24 months unless an ongoing commercial engagement requires otherwise. Deletion may be requested at any time by contacting us directly.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared with email service providers bound by appropriate data processing agreements, or with regulatory authorities where legally required.

6. Google Fonts

This site uses Google Fonts, a typography service provided by Google LLC. When the site loads, your browser may transmit technical data — including your IP address — to Google servers to retrieve font files. We do not use analytics cookies or any tracking technologies on this website. For Google’s data practices, see policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis for processing. Submit requests to office@pushanenterprise.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are maintained to protect personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. No method of internet transmission is entirely secure; we take our obligations seriously and review our practices regularly.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
