Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2026

Pushan Enterprise (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to the protection of personal information in accordance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Pushan Enterprise is a Strategic Market Linkage and Supply Chain Advisory firm registered and operating in Uganda.

  • Address: Plot 104–114 Butabika Road, Kampala, Uganda
  • Contact: office@pushanenterprise.com

2. Data collected

We collect personal data only when you choose to provide it via the enquiry form. This includes name, email address, country of operation, and the content of your message. We collect no sensitive personal data, payment information, or data from persons under 18.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is used solely to respond to enquiries and assess whether Pushan can assist. Our legal basis is legitimate interests — responding to enquiries you have initiated. Where required by law, consent applies.

4. Retention

Enquiry data is retained for a maximum of 24 months unless an ongoing engagement requires otherwise. Deletion may be requested at any time.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data may be shared with email service providers bound by data processing agreements, or with regulatory authorities where legally required.

6. Google Fonts

This site uses Google Fonts. Your browser may transmit technical data (including IP address) to Google servers when loading font files. See Google’s Privacy Policy. No analytics or tracking cookies are used on this site.

7. Your rights

Under applicable law you have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. Contact us at office@pushanenterprise.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are maintained to protect personal data. No internet transmission is entirely secure; we take our obligations seriously.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. The date above reflects the most recent revision.
