The model

No trade.
High value.

Pushan operates in the space between exceptional produce and the markets that can truly recognise it. We do not buy or sell. We construct the infrastructure — the relationships, the compliance, the market positioning — that elevates a cooperative from commodity seller to preferred global supplier.

Our remuneration is a performance-based Market Linkage Fee, earned solely upon execution of a successful international sale. Full disclosure of buyer profiles, pricing, and contract terms is standard. The cooperative retains complete ownership of its product throughout.

The farmer

From the soil.
To the world.

The distance between a farmer and an international market is not kilometres. It is knowledge, organisation, and access. We walk that distance alongside every cooperative — from the first conversation to the first contract, and every stage beyond.

Farming practices

Quality is built before the harvest. We guide the agronomic practices that produce the cup profiles premium international buyers actively seek.

Group formation & cooperative structuring

Individual farmers cannot access international markets alone. We support cooperative formation — converting individual growers into a credible, export-ready supply group.

Traceability & quality verification

The cooperative makes the coffee traceable. The farmer maintains the standard. The buyer receives verified product — cupping scores, moisture reports, full lot documentation.

Export process & EUDR compliance

Documentation, certification, deforestation regulation compliance — the administrative landscape that separates commodity pricing from premium contracts. Navigated completely.

Logistics & supply chain

Farm gate to container to destination port — understood, coordinated, and documented. Full traceability to destination is available at cost.

Training & capability building

Practical, ongoing, never theoretical. The goal is not dependency — it is a cooperative that fully commands its own value chain.

Variety preservation & agroforestry

Uganda holds rare and heritage coffee varieties at risk of being lost. We work with cooperatives to protect them and promote land stewardship that sustains production for generations.

Carbon revenue generation

Shade canopies within cooperative land hold carbon value that has gone unmonetised. We identify eligible areas and connect cooperatives to verified carbon programmes — a second revenue stream for environmental stewardship already being practised.

Origin coffee

Exceptional origin.
Verified character.

Uganda holds a position of particular significance in the world of specialty coffee — Arabica from the highland slopes of Mt. Elgon and Rwenzori, shaped by altitude, volcanic soil, and generations of craft. These are not commodity lots. They are provenance stories, verifiable from farm to cup.

Ugandan Arabica

High-altitude. Complex. Irreplaceable.

Grown above 1,500m across four distinct agro-ecological zones, each with a flavour identity shaped by its geography, microclimate, and the communities who tend it. Profiled by the Uganda Coffee Development Authority.
