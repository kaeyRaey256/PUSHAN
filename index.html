The farmer

From the soil.
To the world.

The distance between an individual farmer and an international market is not measured in kilometres. It is measured in knowledge, organisation, and access. Pushan walks that distance alongside the farmer — from the first step to the last.

Farming practices

Quality begins before the harvest. We guide cooperatives on the agronomic practices that produce the cup profiles international buyers seek — variety selection, harvest discipline, and post-harvest handling.

Group formation & cooperative structuring

Individual farmers cannot access international markets alone. We support the formation and strengthening of cooperatives — converting individual growers into a credible, export-ready supplier group.

Traceability & quality verification

The cooperative makes the coffee traceable. The farmer adheres to the standard. The buyer receives a verified product. We build the documentation chain — cupping scores, moisture reports, lot references — that international buyers require.

Export process & EUDR compliance

Documentation, certification, and formal export channels — the administrative landscape that separates commodity pricing from premium contracts. We guide cooperatives through it completely, including European market compliance under current deforestation regulations.

Logistics & supply chain

From farm gate to container to destination port. The intricacies of inland movement, port handling, and international freight — understood, navigated, and coordinated. Full traceability to destination, available at cost.

Training & capability building

Practical, ongoing, never theoretical. Cooperatives who understand the value chain they operate within command it. The goal is not dependency — it is mastery of the entire process.

Variety preservation & agroforestry

Uganda holds rare and heritage coffee varieties that risk being lost. We work with cooperatives to preserve these varieties and promote agroforestry practices that sustain the land for the generations who will farm it next.

Carbon revenue generation

Shade canopies within cooperative land hold carbon value that has gone unmonetised. We identify eligible areas and connect cooperatives to verified carbon programmes — turning environmental stewardship into a second revenue stream.

Ugandan Arabica

High-altitude. Complex. Irreplaceable.

Grown above 1,500m across four distinct agro-ecological zones — each with a flavour identity shaped by its geography, microclimate, and the communities who tend it. Profiled by the Uganda Coffee Development Authority.
