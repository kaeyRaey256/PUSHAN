Uganda's Coffee

Uganda produces some of the most distinct coffees on the continent — Arabica from the highland slopes of Mt. Elgon and Rwenzori, shaped by altitude, volcanic soil, and generations of craft. Every region has a story the cup tells.

Our sister brand, BushBucks Coffee ↗, is built on these beans — hand-picked, artisan-roasted, traceable from farm to cup. It is the Pushan model in practice.

Ugandan Arabica

High-altitude. Complex. Distinctive.

Uganda's Arabica grows on the highland slopes above 1,500m — where altitude, ambient temperature, and volcanic soil combine to produce flavour profiles that specialty roasters in Europe, Asia, and the US actively seek.

Green Bean Sourcing

We source green beans directly.

For buyers who want to work directly with Ugandan origin — we facilitate access to verified cooperative supply, with full documentation and quality verification at every stage.

  • Moisture and density reports
  • SCA cupping scores
  • Traceability documentation from cooperative to Mombasa
  • Onward tracking to destination port — coordinated at buyer's cost
  • Photo documentation and lot references

Micro-lots from 1–2MT and cooperative lots from 5–20MT available. Processing styles: washed, natural, honey, and experimental fermentation.

How it works

Three steps.
No risk.
No upfront cost.

Transparent by design. Once the link is made, you are never dependent on us again.

1

We understand your position

What you grow, how much you can supply, what your cooperative needs from a market. No two groups are the same and we do not treat them that way.

2

We find the right match

Using our network built over 19 years in the Ugandan coffee sector, we identify verified buyers whose needs align with yours — in volume, quality, certification, and timing.

3

We make the introduction

We support the early stages of the relationship. When it is solid, we step back. You keep the connection, the contract, and every shilling of the trade.

★

Our fee is agreed in advance and paid only when a profitable commercial contract is secured. That is our commitment to every cooperative we work with.

Beyond the link

We don't just open doors.
We help you walk through them.

Many of the cooperatives we work with have asked the same thing: how do we understand this process ourselves? That question is one we take seriously.

01
📈

Revenue optimisation

Understanding what your coffee is actually worth on the international market — not the farm gate price, not the middleman price. The real premium. Quality tiering, certification value, origin storytelling, and how to position your cooperative's produce to command the return it deserves.

02
📋

The export process

Many cooperatives have never exported directly — and the documentation, verification, and formal channels involved can feel out of reach. We demystify it. What is required. How quality verification works. How formal export channels unlock better pricing. Clearly, in plain language.

03
🚛

Logistics & traceability

From farm gate to container to destination port. Understanding road and rail options, what traceability from source to buyer means in practice, and how moisture reports, cupping documentation, and chain-of-custody records make your produce more valuable — not just more compliant.

When Ugandan cooperatives export well, Uganda earns. We are proud to be part of that.
